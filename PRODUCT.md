# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite, TypeScript and Three.js with no UI framework, confirmed by the owner. Replaces the Angular 11 app. Hosted on Cloudflare as a static build.

## Purpose

Paul Giron's personal site. A calling card, not a portfolio: one fullscreen page that works as an art piece, states the name and lets anyone get in touch.

## Users

Anyone curious about Paul: peers, friends, people who came across the name. They arrive from a link, usually with no task beyond seeing who this is, on desktop or phone.

## Content

- The name, "Paul Giron".
- One contact button that opens a Tally form. The form does not exist yet, so its ID stays a single setting to fill in.
- Nothing else: no job title, projects, bio or navigation.

## Constraints

- No scrolling. Fullscreen on every device.
- Must be 3D in the browser.
- The name and the contact action stay available when WebGL or WebGPU is missing, and under reduced motion.

## Brand commitments

Elegant, fun-ish, modern, in the owner's words: "like an art poc".
